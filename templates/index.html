GETTING STARTED

Quick guide.

A simple workflow for making your first video.

01

Create a project

Give your video a title and describe the topic you want to cover.

02

Draft your script

Use the offline script starter, then edit the narration to sound like you.

03

Build scenes

Split your story into scenes. Upload a visual or let RedPit create a branded title card.

04

Add audio

Upload a voiceover and optional music track in MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG or AAC.

05

Render locally

Install FFmpeg on your computer, then render a 1080 × 1920 MP4.

06

Download & publish

Download the MP4 and upload it to your favourite social platform.

About the free AI features

The built-in script starter works offline using templates. It is not a full AI model. You can later connect a local model such as Ollama, but a 4GB RAM computer may be too limited for reliable model inference. No paid API key is required for the core editor and renderer.
